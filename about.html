ABOUT SOFOF TECH IT FARM

Engineered for Visionaries.
Rooted in Dhaka, Delivering to the World.

Founded with a belief that elite technical talent can bridge continents, SOFOF TECH has grown into a trusted engineering partner for enterprises and scale-ups in Saudi Arabia, the United States, Europe, and Asia.

Our Journey

Not Just Building Software—Shaping the Next Frontier of AI & Engineering

At SOFOF TECH IT FARM, our mission is to eliminate the friction, latency, and astronomical expense of building high-performance software. We combine seasoned senior full-stack engineers with state-of-the-art AI tooling to compress multi-quarter roadmaps into bi-weekly production releases.

From coordinating the high-stakes pilgrimage water supply of Mecca in Qatarat, to developing cardless palm biometric POS payments in Salam Pay, our team thrives when challenges are complex and downtime is unacceptable.
